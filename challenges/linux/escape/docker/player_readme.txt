Before you start poking around blindly, take a moment
to understand what you're actually working with.

🎭 Linux capabilities
Linux capabilities are often misunderstood - and
misconfigured. A process (or a user) can be granted
powerful capabilities like CAP_NET_ADMIN, CAP_SYS_ADMIN,
or CAP_DAC_OVERRIDE without anyone realizing the
blast radius.

exemple :
    capsh --print



Don't waste your first hour chasing a capability that
leads nowhere.


**********************HINT***************************
 LOOK OUTWARD, NOT JUST INWARD
*****************************************************

Once you've found your neighbors, ask yourself: what
services are they running, and do any of them still use
default or development-grade credentials? Production
hardening is often the first thing to slip in a rush to
ship.

Good luck. The flag isn't here - but the path to it is.
