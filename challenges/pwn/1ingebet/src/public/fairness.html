Provably Fair™

1ingeBet is certified provably fair. At the start of your session we generate one random 16-bit seed and show it to you, right there in the UI. Every round's seed is derived from it by a fixed, public increment, and every round reshuffles a freshly built 52-card deck with a textbook Fisher–Yates shuffle. Nothing is hidden from you. Nothing can go wrong.
