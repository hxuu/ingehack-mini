Advice sheet 4

Refunds and transfers

  1. Are transfers safe?

    Yes. The server signs the session cookie, and that signature protects every transfer. If anyone changes the sign-in details, the server notices and refuses the request, so a transfer can only come from an account that has signed in.

  2. Can a student approve their own refund?

    No. Only a minha account can approve a refund, and the console checks the account before it moves any money.

  3. Do I need to do anything before I share a link?

    No. Open the link in the usual browser and the browser uses the minha account automatically.

  4. Who do I contact?

    The faculty office on the ground floor, or the minha officer at the counter between 08:00 and 16:00.
