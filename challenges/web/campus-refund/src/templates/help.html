Advice sheet 4

Refunds and transfers

  1. Are transfers safe?

    Yes. Every transfer is protected because the session cookie is signed. If anyone changed the sign-in details, the server would notice and refuse the request, so a transfer can only come from an account that has signed in.

  2. Can a student approve their own refund?

    No. Only a bursary account can approve a refund, and the console checks the account before it moves any money.

  3. Do I need to do anything before I share a link?

    No. Open the link in the usual browser and the bursary's account is used automatically.

  4. Who do I contact?

    The faculty office on the second floor, or the bursar at the counter between ten and four.
